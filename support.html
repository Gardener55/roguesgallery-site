Support

We're here to help.

Having trouble, found a bug, or have an idea for a new game or feature? We'd love to hear from you.

Contact us

Email support@roguesgallery.app and we'll get back to you. To help us help you faster, please include:

  • Your device model and iOS or Android version
  • Which game you were playing
  • A description of what happened (and a screenshot if you have one)

Frequently asked

Is the app free?

Yes — The Rogue's Gallery is free to download and play on the App Store and Google Play. Some optional extras and ads help support development.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play right away with no sign-up. Your progress is saved on your device.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every game plays fully offline. Stats and coins earned offline sync to your account automatically once you reconnect.

Is it on Android?

Yes — The Rogue's Gallery is on Google Play for Android phones and tablets, as well as on the App Store for iPhone and iPad.
