How to Play Clear the Dungeon

In Clear the Dungeon, the twelve face cards are monsters lined up three rows deep, and the rest of the deck is your arsenal. Wear each monster down, then land the finishing blow with a card of its suit, before your cards run out.

Game design: Mark S. Ball. Credited with gratitude; the designer is not affiliated with The Rogue’s Gallery.

  • Players1
  • Deck52 cards + 2 Jokers
  • Monsters12
  • GoalDefeat every monster

Setup

  • The dungeon: the 12 Jacks, Queens and Kings, shuffled into 3 rows of 4. Only the front row starts face up.
  • Your power deck: the Aces through 10s plus 2 Jokers (42 cards). Draw 3.

Defeating a monster

  1. Place a first damage card on a face-up monster. Any card works.
  2. Place a second damage card. The two together must reach the monster's health.
  3. Place a finisher: a card of the monster's suit, or a Joker. The monster is defeated, and the monster behind it is revealed.

Aces count as 1, number cards at face value, and Jokers as 10. Jokers are wild finishers.

Turns

Each turn you hold 3 cards. Place what you can, then end the turn: unused cards are discarded and you draw 3 more. Cards already on monsters stay there between turns.

You win by defeating all 12 monsters. If your power deck runs out first, you lose.

Strategy tips

  • Work on several monsters at once. A big card that doesn't fit one monster may set up another.
  • Save Jokers for awkward suits, when you're holding no finisher for a monster that's otherwise ready.
  • Don't overspend. A 10 and a 9 on a Jack wastes damage you'll want later.

Frequently asked questions

What is a finisher in Clear the Dungeon?

The third card on a monster, which must match its suit (or be a Joker) to defeat it once the first two cards reach its health.

Do cards on monsters carry over between turns?

Yes. Damage stays on a monster until it's defeated.
