How to Play Beehive Solitaire

Beehive is a relaxed matching solitaire. Instead of building suits in order, you gather cards of the same rank until you have four of a kind, while emptying the beehive pile along the way.

  • Players1
  • Deck52 cards
  • Garden6 spaces
  • Goal13 sets of four of a kind

Setup

  • The Beehive: 10 cards in a pile. Only the top card can be played.
  • The Garden: 6 cards face up. This is where sets are built.
  • The deck: the remaining 36 cards.

Playing

  • Stack Garden cards of the same rank onto each other.
  • Play the top Beehive card or top waste card onto a matching Garden card.
  • Deal three cards at a time from the deck to the waste pile. When the deck runs out, turn the waste over and keep going.
  • Fill an empty Garden space with the top Beehive card. Once the Beehive is empty, you can fill spaces from the waste instead.
  • Four cards of the same rank complete a set, which is removed and frees its space.

Winning and losing

You win when all 52 cards are in 13 completed sets. You lose if you go through the entire deck without making a single match.

Strategy tips

  • Empty the Beehive first. It's the only pile with cards you can't reach any other way.
  • Don't fill every Garden space at once. An open space gives the next Beehive card somewhere to go.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Beehive in Beehive solitaire?

A pile of ten cards where only the top card is available. Emptying it is the first big step towards a win.

Is Beehive solitaire hard?

It's one of the more relaxed solitaire games, since you collect matching ranks rather than building suits in order.
