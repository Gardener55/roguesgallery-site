How to Play Solitaire

Klondike is the solitaire most people mean when they say "solitaire": seven columns, a stock pile, and four foundations to build from Ace to King. Here's how to play it, plus a few habits that win more games.

  • Players1
  • Deck52 cards
  • ModesDraw 1 or Draw 3
  • GoalBuild all four suits, Ace to King

Setup

  • Tableau: deal 7 columns. The first column has 1 card, the second 2, and so on up to 7. Only the top card of each column is face up.
  • Stock: the remaining 24 cards sit face down.
  • Foundations: four empty piles, one per suit.

Legal moves

  • Stack cards on the tableau in descending order, alternating colors: a black 8 on a red 9.
  • Move a face-up card together with everything on top of it as one group.
  • When you move the last face-up card off a column, flip the next card face up.
  • Only a King (or a run starting with a King) can fill an empty column.
  • Build each foundation in suit from Ace up to King.

The stock: Draw 1 or Draw 3

Turn cards from the stock onto the waste pile. Only the top waste card can be played. When the stock runs out, flip the waste pile over to go through it again.

  • Draw 1: turn one card at a time. The easier mode.
  • Draw 3: turn three at a time, and only the top one is playable. Harder, because some cards stay out of reach.

Strategy tips

  • Uncover face-down cards first. Prefer the move that flips a hidden card, especially in the longest columns.
  • Don't empty a column without a King to put there.
  • Don't rush cards to the foundations. A low card on the tableau may be the place you need to park a card of the other color.
  • Use the stock last. Make every tableau move you can before drawing.

Frequently asked questions

What is Klondike solitaire?

The classic solitaire game with seven tableau columns, a stock pile and four foundations, where you build each suit from Ace to King.

What is the difference between Draw 1 and Draw 3?

In Draw 1 you turn one card from the stock at a time. In Draw 3 you turn three and can only play the top one, which makes the game harder.

What can go in an empty column in solitaire?

Only a King, or a sequence that starts with a King.
