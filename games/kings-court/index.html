How to Play King's Court

King's Court is a pairing solitaire. Find two cards that add up to eleven, cover them, and keep going until a royal sits on top of every one of the twelve piles.

  • Players1
  • Deck52 cards
  • Layout12 piles
  • GoalA face card on every pile

Setup

Deal 12 cards face up in a 3×4 grid. The rest of the deck is the draw pile.

Playing

  • Find two piles whose top cards add up to exactly 11. Aces count as 1.
  • Pick both: a new card from the draw pile is dealt onto each.
  • When a Jack, Queen or King lands on a pile, that pile is crowned and locked.

Valid pairs: A+10, 2+9, 3+8, 4+7, 5+6.

Lifeline

If a pile holds just one card and that card is a face card, you can tap it to send it to the bottom of the draw pile and deal a new card in its place.

Winning and losing

You win when all 12 piles are crowned. You lose when no two top cards add up to 11 and no lifeline is available.

Strategy tips

  • Look at all the pairs before choosing. Taking one pair can leave another without a partner.
  • Keep the 5s and 6s in mind. Their pairs are the easiest to break up by accident.

Frequently asked questions

What adds up to 11 in King's Court?

Ace and 10, 2 and 9, 3 and 8, 4 and 7, and 5 and 6. Aces count as 1.

What happens when a face card is dealt in King's Court?

That pile is crowned and locked. Crown all twelve to win.
