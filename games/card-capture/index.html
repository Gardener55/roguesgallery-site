How to Play Card Capture

Card Capture is a solo deckbuilder played with a standard deck. You start with a weak hand of 2s, 3s and 4s and grow stronger by capturing the enemy cards you beat. Capture all sixteen face cards and Aces to win.

Game design: Lucas Gentry. Credited with gratitude; the designer is not affiliated with The Rogue’s Gallery.

  • Players1
  • Deck52 cards + 2 Jokers
  • Starting deck14 cards
  • GoalCapture all 16 face cards and Aces

Setup

  • Your draw deck: the 2s, 3s and 4s of every suit plus two Jokers (14 cards).
  • The enemy deck: the other 40 cards (5 to Ace). Four of them form the enemy row; the first row holds number cards only.

Card values: number cards at face value, Jack 11, Queen 12, King 13, Ace 14.

A round

  1. Enemy phase: enemy cards slide right and new ones fill the row from the left.
  2. Discard phase: you may discard cards you can't use.
  3. Draw phase: draw until you hold 4. If your draw deck is empty, shuffle your discard pile to make a new one.
  4. Capture phase: take exactly one of the actions below.

Actions

  • Capture: spend cards of the enemy's suit whose total is at least its value. The enemy and your spent cards go to your discard pile, so the enemy joins your deck.
  • Yield: the rightmost enemy and one card from your hand are removed for good. If that enemy is a face card or Ace, you lose.
  • Sacrifice: remove two of your cards for good and send any one enemy to the bottom of the enemy deck.

Jokers double the value of one other card you're playing. For example, 2♥ + 4♥ + Joker on the 4 makes 10.

Winning and losing

You win once all 16 face cards and Aces are in your deck. You lose if any of them is removed for good, or if your deck is too thin to go on.

Strategy tips

  • Capture mid-value cards early. 8s, 9s and 10s make the face cards reachable later.
  • Watch the right end of the row. If a face card is about to be forced out by a Yield, deal with it or sacrifice to send it back.
  • Thin out weak cards. Sacrificing 2s makes your strong cards come round more often.

Frequently asked questions

What is Card Capture?

A solo deckbuilding card game played with a standard deck, where you capture enemy cards to make your own deck stronger.

How does the Joker work in Card Capture?

It doubles the value of one other card in the same play. It can't be played by itself.
