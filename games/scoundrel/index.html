How to Play Scoundrel

Scoundrel is a single-player dungeon crawl played with an ordinary deck of cards. The deck is the dungeon: black cards are monsters, diamonds are weapons, hearts are potions, and you have 20 health to make it out alive. This guide explains the rules as they're played in The Rogue's Gallery.

Game design: Zach Gage & Kurt Bieg. Credited with gratitude; the designer is not affiliated with The Rogue’s Gallery.

  • Players1
  • Deck44 cards
  • TimeAbout 10 minutes
  • GoalGet through the dungeon alive

Setup

Take a standard 52-card deck and remove the Jokers, the red face cards and the red Aces (the Jack, Queen, King and Ace of hearts and of diamonds). That leaves 44 cards. Shuffle them and place them face down: this pile is the dungeon.

You start with 20 health, which is also the most you can ever have.

What each card does

CardsRoleEffect
♣ Clubs and ♠ Spades (2 to Ace)MonstersDeal damage equal to their value. Jack = 11, Queen = 12, King = 13, Ace = 14.
♦ Diamonds (2 to 10)WeaponsEquip one to reduce the damage monsters deal.
♥ Hearts (2 to 10)PotionsRestore health equal to their value, up to 20.

Playing a room

Each turn, deal cards from the dungeon until four are face up in front of you. These four cards are the room. You then have two choices:

  1. Face the room. Pick three of the four cards, one at a time, and resolve each one. The fourth card stays on the table and becomes the first card of the next room.
  2. Run. Before you take any card, you can flee: all four cards go to the bottom of the dungeon and you deal a fresh room. You can't run from two rooms in a row.

Fighting monsters

  • Bare-handed: you take the monster's full value as damage.
  • With a weapon: you take the monster's value minus the weapon's value (never less than zero). A 5♦ against a Jack (11) costs you 6 health.
  • Weapons wear down. Once a weapon has killed a monster, it can only be used on monsters weaker than the last one it killed. Kill a 9 with your weapon, and from then on it only works against 8 or lower. You can always fight bare-handed instead.
  • Picking up a new weapon replaces your current one, wear and all.

If your health reaches 0, the run is over.

Drinking potions

A potion heals you by its value, but health never goes above 20. Only the first potion you take in a room heals. A second potion in the same room is simply discarded.

How to win

The Rogue's Gallery offers two difficulties:

  • Easy: you escape once the dungeon can no longer fill a room.
  • Hard: you must use every card in the dungeon to escape.

A sample room

You have 20 health and no weapon. The room is 7♠, 5♦, 9♥, J♣.

  1. Take the 5♦ first. You now have a 5 weapon.
  2. Fight the J♣ with it: 11 − 5 = 6 damage. You're at 14.
  3. Fight the 7♠. Your weapon last killed an 11, so it still works on a 7: 7 − 5 = 2 damage. You're at 12.
  4. Leave the 9♥ on the table. It carries into the next room, where it can heal you for the full 9.

Had you killed the 7 first, your weapon couldn't touch the Jack, and it would have cost you 11 health bare-handed.

Strategy tips

  • Use a fresh weapon on the biggest monster first. Every kill limits what the weapon can hit next, so work from the largest monster down.
  • Choose the card you leave behind. The fourth card follows you into the next room. Leaving a potion there when you're near full health saves it for when you need it.
  • Don't waste healing. Only one potion per room works, and anything over 20 is lost.
  • Run from the worst rooms. Two big monsters and no weapon is a good time to flee. But you can't run twice in a row, so save it for when it counts.
  • A small weapon can beat a worn one. If your 10 now only works on 3s, a fresh 4 may serve you better.

Daily Dungeon and Duels

The Rogue's Gallery adds two competitive ways to play Scoundrel:

  • Daily Dungeon: one dungeon a day, with the same deck for every player. You get one attempt with no undo, then see where you rank on the daily leaderboard.
  • Duels: play the same deck as one other player with 8 minutes on the clock. You score 10 points for every card cleared and 2 for every point of monster slain, plus 20 for each health point left if you escape and a bonus for speed. Win to climb your Duel Rank from Nine to Ace.

Frequently asked questions

How many cards are in a Scoundrel deck?

44. Start with a standard 52-card deck and remove the Jokers, the red face cards and the red Aces.

Who created Scoundrel?

Scoundrel was designed by Zach Gage and Kurt Bieg.

Can you run from two rooms in a row?

No. After running from a room you must face the next one.

Are Aces high or low in Scoundrel?

High. The black Aces are the strongest monsters, worth 14.

Can you play Scoundrel on your phone?

Yes. Scoundrel is one of the twelve free card games in The Rogue's Gallery for iPhone, iPad and Android.
