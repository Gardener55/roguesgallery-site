How to Play Aces High

Aces High is a quick, punishing solitaire, known elsewhere as Aces Up. Four columns, one simple rule for removing cards, and a single goal: finish with the four Aces on top. Winning it is rare enough to feel great.

  • Players1
  • Deck52 cards
  • Layout4 columns
  • GoalGet all four Aces on top

Setup

Deal four cards face up in a row. Each one starts a column. The rest of the deck stays face down for dealing.

Removing cards

Look at the top card of each column. If two top cards share a suit, the lower one can be removed. Aces are high, so nothing removes an Ace.

Example: the 4♥ and the J♥ are both showing. Remove the 4♥.

Moving cards

Whenever a column is empty, you can move any top card into it. Use this to dig out the cards trapped underneath.

Dealing

When you're out of moves, deal four new cards, one onto each column, and keep going until the deck is used up.

Trophies

TrophyHow to earn it
BronzeGet all four Aces on top at any point.
SilverFinish the deck with the four Aces on top.
GoldFinish with only the four Aces left on the table.

Earning Bronze doesn't end the game, so you can keep playing for Silver or Gold. In The Rogue's Gallery you can also spend coins once per game on a Shadow Slip, which moves any card to any column.

Strategy tips

  • Empty columns are everything. Spend them on the card that frees the most buried cards, not the first move you see.
  • Get Aces to the bottom of a column. An Ace with nothing under it never blocks anything again.
  • Make every removal before dealing. Each deal buries the top cards.

Frequently asked questions

Is Aces High the same as Aces Up?

Yes. Aces High is The Rogue's Gallery's version of the solitaire also called Aces Up or Idiot's Delight, with a trophy system added.

Are Aces high or low in Aces High?

High. An Ace can never be removed, which is why the goal is to finish with all four on top.
