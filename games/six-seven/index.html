Classic · French Solitaire

How to Play 6-7

6-7 is a single-pass solitaire played on a 3×3 grid. Sevens climb to Kings in the corners, sixes count down to Aces in the center, and you only go through the deck once.

  • Players1
  • Deck52 cards
  • Passes1, no redeals
  • GoalAll 52 cards on the foundations

The grid

  • Corners (4): build up from 7: 7, 8, 9, 10, J, Q, K. Rank only; suits don't matter.
  • Center: build down from 6: 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, A. After an Ace, another 6 can start again, so all four runs pass through the center.
  • Sides (4): free cells. Each holds one card while you wait for it to fit.

Playing

  • Draw one card at a time. Play it to a foundation, store it in an empty side cell, or leave it on the discard pile.
  • Every 6 you draw goes to a Saved 6s reserve. Move one to the center whenever the center is empty or showing an Ace.
  • You can draw at any time; no move is ever forced.
  • The deck is used once. When it's gone and nothing more can be played, the game ends.

Strategy tips

  • Keep a side cell open for the card that's about to be needed.
  • Play from the discard pile before drawing. Buried discards are lost for good.
  • Count what's coming. With one pass, knowing which ranks are still in the deck helps you decide what to store.

Frequently asked questions

Can you go through the deck more than once in 6-7?

No. 6-7 is a single-pass game with no redeals.

Do suits matter in 6-7 solitaire?

No. The foundations are built by rank only.
