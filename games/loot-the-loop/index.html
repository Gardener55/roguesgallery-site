How to Play Loot the Loop

Loot the Loop turns the deck into a temple that loops back on itself. Cards cycle from the top to the bottom as you explore, so the same trap can come round again. Find all four Aces, avoid the face cards and get out through the exit.

Game design: Wil Su. Credited with gratitude; not affiliated with The Rogue’s Gallery.

  • Players1
  • Deck52 cards + 1 Joker
  • Saved pathsUp to 3
  • GoalCollect 4 Aces, then reach the Joker

Setup

Shuffle the 52 cards and one Joker into a single face-down pile: the loop. Turn the top two cards face up.

What the cards mean

CardMeaning
2 to 10Paths. Use them to move through the loop, or save them for later.
AcesArtifacts. You need all four.
Jack, Queen, KingTraps. Landing on one that's face up ends the game.
JokerThe exit. Land on it with all four Aces to win.

Your actions

  • Look around: turn the top two cards face up.
  • Explore: pick a face-up number card among the top two. Move that many cards from the top of the loop to the bottom, and you land on whatever card is now on top.
  • Mark a path: set aside a face-up number card from the top two. You can hold up to three.
  • Return a path: put a saved card back on top of the loop.

Where you land

  • A face-down card: safe. It's turned face up.
  • A face-up number card: you collect it as loot.
  • A face-up Ace: you collect it as an artifact.
  • A face-up Jack, Queen or King: it's a trap and the game is over.
  • The Joker: you escape and win, if you hold all four Aces.

You also lose if you're stuck: both top cards are face up, neither is a number card, and you have no saved paths.

Strategy tips

  • Keep two cards revealed. You can't plan a move you can't see.
  • Remember where the traps are. A face-up trap stays deadly as it cycles around.
  • Landing face down is always safe. When in doubt, aim for an unseen card.
  • Bank paths for emergencies. A saved card is your way out when the top two cards are traps.

Frequently asked questions

How do you win Loot the Loop?

Collect all four Aces by landing on them face up, then land on the Joker exit.

Is landing on a face card always a loss?

Only if it's face up. Landing on a face-down card is always safe, even if it turns out to be a trap.
