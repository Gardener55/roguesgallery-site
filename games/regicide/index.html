How to Play Regicide

In Regicide you fight your way through the royal court with a standard deck of cards. Twelve Jacks, Queens and Kings stand between you and the throne, each tougher than the last, and every card you play is both an attack and a defense. Regicide began as a cooperative game for up to four players; this guide covers the solo rules as played in The Rogue's Gallery.

Game design: Badgers From Mars (Paul Abrahams, Luke Badger & Andy Richdale). Credited with gratitude; the designer is not affiliated with The Rogue’s Gallery.

  • Players1
  • Deck52 cards
  • Hand size8 cards
  • GoalDefeat all 12 royals

Setup

  • The castle: the 12 face cards are the enemies. You face the Jacks first, then the Queens, then the Kings.
  • The tavern deck: the Aces through 10s of every suit (40 cards) are your cards. Shuffle them and draw a hand of 8.
  • Jokers: you have 2 Jokers to use during the game.

A turn

  1. Play a card (or a legal combo) from your hand to attack the current enemy.
  2. Trigger its suit power (see below).
  3. Deal damage equal to the card's value. If the enemy's health reaches 0, it's defeated and the next royal steps up.
  4. Survive the counterattack. If the enemy is still standing, it attacks. Discard cards from your hand whose values add up to at least its attack. If you can't, you lose.

Suit powers

SuitPower
♥ HeartsShuffle your discard pile and put that many cards back under the tavern deck.
♦ DiamondsDraw that many cards (your hand holds 8 at most).
♣ ClubsDeal double damage.
♠ SpadesReduce the enemy's attack by that much for the rest of the fight.

Enemies are immune to their own suit. A Jack of Clubs takes normal damage from clubs, but not double.

Card values, combos and Jokers

  • Aces count as 1 and 2 to 10 count at face value. A royal you add to your deck is worth 10 (Jack), 15 (Queen) or 20 (King).
  • Combos: play two or more cards of the same rank together if their total is 10 or less (such as 2-2-2-2, 3-3-3 or 5-5). Every suit in the combo triggers its power, using the combo's total value.
  • Animal companions: an Ace can be paired with any one other card.
  • Exact kill: bring an enemy to exactly 0 health and it joins your deck instead of being discarded.
  • Jokers: discard your whole hand and draw back up to 8. You only get two, and using fewer earns a better victory: Gold for none, Silver for one, Bronze for two.

Strategy tips

  • Plan for the counterattack. Before attacking, make sure the cards left in your hand can still absorb the enemy's hit.
  • Use spades early in a fight. Their reduction lasts until the enemy falls, so it protects you on every following turn.
  • Aim for exact kills. A royal in your deck is a powerful card later, especially against Kings.
  • Watch the enemy's suit. A clubs attack on a club royal loses its doubling, and hearts against a heart royal won't refill your deck.
  • Hold a Joker for a dead hand, not just a weak one. A Gold victory means never using one.

Frequently asked questions

How many royals do you have to beat in Regicide?

Twelve: the four Jacks, four Queens and four Kings.

What does an exact kill do in Regicide?

If your attack brings an enemy to exactly 0 health, that royal joins your deck as a strong card instead of being discarded.

Can enemies use their own suit's power?

Enemies are immune to the suit power that matches their own suit, though your card still deals its normal damage.

Who designed Regicide?

Regicide was designed by Badgers From Mars: Paul Abrahams, Luke Badger and Andy Richdale.

How many Jokers do you get in solo Regicide?

Two. Each one discards your hand and draws you back up to 8 cards.
