How to Play Accordion

Accordion is an old patience game with one of the simplest rule sets in solitaire, and one of the hardest wins. Lay the cards in a line and fold them together, like the instrument, until as few piles as possible remain.

  • Players1
  • Deck52 cards
  • Win5 piles or fewer
  • Perfect game1 pile

Setup

Deal the cards face up in a line from left to right, either one at a time as you play or all 52 at once. Each card is a pile.

Legal moves

A pile can move onto another pile if their top cards share a suit or a rank, but only to:

  • the pile immediately to its left, or
  • the pile three places to its left.

The whole pile moves as one, and its top card is now the top of the new pile. The gap closes up.

Line: 7♥ · K♠ · 2♣ · 7♣. The 7♣ can jump three places onto the 7♥ (same rank), or one place onto the 2♣ (same suit).

Winning

Piles leftResult
1Gold trophy
2Silver trophy
3Bronze trophy
4 or 5Win

In The Rogue's Gallery you can also spend coins once per game on Sleight of Hand, which swaps two neighboring piles.

Strategy tips

  • Look before you leap. A move often creates a new match further down the line; play the one that sets up a chain.
  • Compare both jumps. When a pile can move one place or three, each leaves different cards side by side. Pick the one that creates the next match.
  • Keep variety on top. Piles topped by common suits and ranks match more easily.

Frequently asked questions

How hard is it to win Accordion solitaire?

Getting down to one pile is very rare. That's why The Rogue's Gallery counts five piles or fewer as a win and gives trophies for 3, 2 and 1.

Can you move a pile two places to the left in Accordion?

No. Only one place or three places to the left.
