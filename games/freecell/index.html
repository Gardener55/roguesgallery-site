How to Play FreeCell

FreeCell is the solitaire game where nothing is hidden. Every card is dealt face up, so almost every deal can be won with careful play. Your four free cells are the key: they hold cards temporarily while you untangle the columns.

Game design: Paul Alfille. Credited with gratitude; the designer is not affiliated with The Rogue’s Gallery.

  • Players1
  • Deck52 cards
  • Layout8 columns, 4 free cells
  • GoalBuild all four suits, Ace to King

Setup

  • Tableau: deal all 52 cards face up into 8 columns: four columns of 7 and four of 6.
  • Free cells: four empty spaces, each holding one card.
  • Foundations: four empty piles, one per suit, built from Ace up to King.

Legal moves

  • Move the bottom card of any column onto another column if it's one rank lower and the opposite color, like a red 6 on a black 7.
  • Move any single card into an empty free cell, and back out again.
  • Move any card, or a properly built sequence, into an empty column.
  • Move a card to its foundation when it's next in line for that suit.

Moving several cards at once

Strictly, you move one card at a time. But a run of cards can be moved together whenever there's enough free space to have moved them one by one. The most cards you can move at once is:

(empty free cells + 1) × 2empty columns

With 2 empty free cells and 1 empty column, you can move (2 + 1) × 2 = 6 cards. Moving into an empty column, that column doesn't count.

How to win

You win when all 52 cards are on the foundations. In The Rogue's Gallery, cards that can safely go up are moved there automatically, and you can take a foundation card back down if you need it.

Strategy tips

  • Free the Aces and 2s early. Look for low cards buried deep and plan the moves that dig them out.
  • Treat free cells as precious. Each one you fill halves your ability to move long sequences. Empty them as fast as you fill them.
  • An empty column is worth more than a free cell. It doubles how many cards you can move.
  • Think before the first move. Every card is visible, so scan the whole layout first.

Frequently asked questions

Who invented FreeCell?

FreeCell was designed by Paul Alfille.

Is every FreeCell game winnable?

Almost. With 52 cards face up, nearly every deal can be solved; only a tiny number of deals are known to be impossible.

How many cards can you move at once in FreeCell?

(empty free cells + 1) multiplied by 2 for each empty column. With 4 empty free cells and no empty columns, that's 5 cards.

What is the difference between FreeCell and Klondike solitaire?

In FreeCell every card is face up and you have four free cells for temporary storage. In Klondike most cards start face down and you draw from a stock pile.
